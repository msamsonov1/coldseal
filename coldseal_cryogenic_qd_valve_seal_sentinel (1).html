Thermo-Mechanical Seal Stiffening & Galling Threshold Dynamics

Quick-Disconnect (QD) fluid couplers operating at orbital propellant depots encounter extreme thermal transitions from $293\text{ K}$ (ambient payload bay) down to $20.3\text{ K}$ (liquid hydrogen). As fluoropolymer seal lips (PTFE/PCTFE/Kel-F) chill, elastic moduli stiffen by up to 500%, while differential thermal contraction shrinks metallic guide bores, fundamentally shifting the required mechanical mating corridor.

1. Cryo Modulus Scaling & Spring-Lip Seating

The required mechanical force $F_{\text{seat}}$ to seat an energized C-ring or spring-energized polymer lip seal increases non-linearly as temperature drops due to glass-state polymer embrittlement and thermal contraction:

$$F_{\text{seat}}(T) = \pi D_s \cdot \sigma_y(T) \cdot w_{\text{contact}} + k_{\text{spring}} \delta_x \cdot \left[1 + \beta \left(\frac{T_0 - T}{T_0}\right)^{1.8}\right]$$

Where $D_s$ is nominal seal diameter ($50.8\text{ mm}$ for 2-inch QD lines), $\sigma_y(T)$ is cryogenic yield stress, $w_{\text{contact}}$ is seal land contact width, and $\beta$ is the empirical polymer vitrification stiffness exponent ($\approx 2.45$ for modified Kel-F).

Room Temp ($293\text{ K}$): $E = 1.4\text{ GPa}$ Liquid H₂ ($20\text{ K}$): $E = 7.2\text{ GPa}$

2. Radial Clearance & Galling Limit

Differential thermal contraction between the Inconel 718 female receptacle sleeve ($\alpha_{\text{inc}} \approx 11 \times 10^{-6}\text{ K}^{-1}$) and 316L stainless male poppet pin ($\alpha_{\text{ss}} \approx 14 \times 10^{-6}\text{ K}^{-1}$) governs radial clearance $\Delta r(T)$:

$$\Delta r(T) = r_0 \left[1 - \int_{T}^{T_0} \alpha_{\text{sleeve}}(T')\,dT'\right] - r_{\text{pin}} \left[1 - \int_{T}^{T_0} \alpha_{\text{pin}}(T')\,dT'\right]$$
$$F_{\text{friction}}(T) = \mu(T) \cdot \frac{E_{\text{eff}}(T) \cdot A_{\text{int}}}{1 - \nu^2} \cdot \left( \frac{\delta_{\text{interference}} - \Delta r(T)}{r_0} \right)$$

If angular misalignment $\theta > 0.4^\circ$, localized Hertzian contact stress spikes past $850\text{ MPa}$, exceeding the critical galling threshold of passivated Inconel guide lands in an inert helium/LH₂ atmosphere.

Critical Galling Stress $\tau_{\text{gall}}$: $720\text{ MPa}$ Max Allowable Tilt: $0.35^\circ$

3. Real-Time Inversion & Anomaly Isolation

The Sentinel algorithm utilizes an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) and online curve parameter estimation to decouple nominal cryo-stiffening from physical mechanical obstructions:

$$\xi(t) = F_{\text{measured}}(t) - \hat{F}_{\text{model}}(T(t), x(t), \dot{x}(t))$$
$$\Phi_{\text{ice}}(t) = \int_{t-\tau}^{t} \left[ \nabla_T \xi \cdot \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} \right] dt \quad \ge \quad \gamma_{\text{threshold}}$$

While nominal cryo mating creates a smooth, continuous rise in $F$ that correlates monotonically with decreasing temperature $T$, foreign cryo-ice accretion produces a distinct discontinuity: high $\frac{dF}{dx}$ at intermediate temperatures ($120\text{ K} - 180\text{ K}$) before the poppet enters its primary detent.

EKF Sampling: 100 Hz Abort Response Latency: $< 12\text{ ms}$

4. Spaceflight Sensor & Hardware Architecture

High-accuracy force/temperature telemetry is acquired at the robotic tool center point (TCP) without penetrating pressure-boundary seals:

  • Temperature Sensor: Platinum resistance thermometer (Pt100/Pt1000 thin film) vacuum-brazed to the poppet collar ($15\text{ K} - 350\text{ K} \pm 0.1\text{ K}$).
  • 6-DOF Load Cell: Cryo-rated foil strain gauge rosette, thermally isolated via Macor ceramic standoff ($\pm 0.2\text{ N}$ resolution up to $2,500\text{ N}$).
  • Displacement Encoding: Dual-channel linear optical encoder ($0.5\ \mu\text{m}$ resolution) integrated into robotic axial pusher screw.
  • Helium Mass Spec Sniffer: Micro-RGA vacuum port monitoring peripheral shroud annulus ($10^{-9}\text{ sccs}$ detection limit).

Orbital Propellant Depots

Physics in 1 Sentence:

Micro-gravity boiloff locks cryogenic seals into unpredictable temperature transients ($20\text{ K} - 150\text{ K}$) that rapidly escalate poppet insertion resistance.

Why Incumbents Missed This:

Legacy spacecraft rely on blind high-torque motorized lead screws that push blindly until a micro-switch trips, frequently galling Inconel seals and leaking tons of scarce propellant.
